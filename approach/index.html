First, I listen. Then, I help make things simpler.

You know your business. I bring a fresh pair of eyes and the technical skill to connect the pieces. We decide together what is worth changing and what is already working well.

FAQ

A few things you might be wondering.

What happens on the free call?

It takes 15 minutes. You tell me what is taking up your time, and we find one thing eating up your week. If I can help, I explain what the next step would look like.

What does it cost?

The first call is free. If it is useful, the next step is a paid mapping session. You can then choose ongoing help if you want it. I can talk you through the price on the call.

How involved do I need to be?

You show me how the work happens today. I set up the fixes with you and explain what I am changing as I go.

Do I have to change the tools I use?

I start with what you already use. If I think something different would help, I explain why and talk it through with you before changing anything.

Can I work with you remotely?

Yes. Most of the work happens over video calls, so it doesn’t matter where you are.
