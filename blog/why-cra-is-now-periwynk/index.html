Why CRA is now Periwynk, and what isn’t changing

Ewetse Chisenga
21 November 2026

If you knew this business as Chisenga Risk & Automation, or CRA, you might have noticed a new name and a new flower on the site. The business is now called Periwynk LLC.

Why the new name

I chose the name because I like the colour. That’s the honest starting point. The more meaningful part is that the periwinkle flower symbolises friendships new and everlasting, and that’s the whole idea behind this business: building a community of trust. That matters even more now, in an age where AI is the big buzzword of the decade and we have people talking big about its impact for better or worse. The reality is it’s going to bring big changes to how people live and work, though in many respects it’s overstated or understated. It’s about navigating these times together, and walking through every uncertain step with people who have your back.

I came to a new place a few years ago not knowing anybody here. In a lot of ways, I was starting from scratch in life. The biggest thing I’ve learned since then is the power of real friendship. That bond can be stronger than a family bond in a lot of cases, as those are the people who chose you rather than being bound by an implicit genetic obligation. I have come to find that there are good people out there who have your best interests at heart, and they are there to be found. Friends can become family, and I don’t take that for granted.

And yes, it’s also my favourite colour. I’d be lying if I said that didn’t play a big part in why I went in this direction. I would have gone for Periwinkle as the name, but the domain costs a fortune, and it turns out my love for the colour has limits. So here we are with Periwynk, which I honestly think works great too.

What isn’t changing

It’s still me, Ewetse Chisenga, based in St. Louis, working one to one with small business owners.

The work is the same. I help owners spend less time on admin, like answering enquiries, chasing quotes and following up with customers. We look at how the work happens now, then I set up the fixes with you, starting with the tools you already use.

The first step is the same too: a free 15-minute call to find one thing eating up your week. If it looks useful, we can go further from there.

My background is the same as well. I studied Actuarial Science, so I look at the numbers: where time and money are being lost, and whether a change actually makes a difference.

What’s new

A new name, a new look, and this blog. I plan to write short posts about where the time goes in a small business, the numbers behind it, and what I learn from talking to owners in person.

If you knew the business as CRA, there’s nothing you need to do. Same person, same way of working, new name.
