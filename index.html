How It Works

One system. Four things it does for you.

60-Second Lead Response

The moment a new lead submits a form, the system sends them a personalized follow-up message before any competitor can call. Your name is in their inbox before they have finished browsing.

Automatic Lead Scoring

Every lead is scored hot, warm, or cold based on how they came in, how they responded, and what they told you. You get a ranked list every morning instead of a pile of names to sort through yourself.

Appointment Booking Without Chasing

Warm leads receive a booking link automatically as part of the follow-up sequence. Qualified prospects put themselves on your calendar without you making a single follow-up call.

Weekly Results Report

Every Monday you receive a report showing leads in, response times, appointments booked, and estimated commission value in the pipeline. The numbers make the return on investment visible every single week.
