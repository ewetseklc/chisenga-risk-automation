What We Build

Services

AI Claims Triage & Reserve Estimation

Automatically classify incoming claims by severity and line of business using LLM-powered analysis. Generate initial reserve estimates in seconds, freeing adjusters to focus on complex cases.

Automated Renewal & Retention Workflows

Trigger multi-step renewal pipelines months before expiry with personalized outreach. Track engagement, escalate non-responders, and sync status back to your AMS automatically.

Intelligent Lead Scoring for Commercial Lines

Enrich inbound leads with firmographic data and score them against your ideal customer profile. Route high-value prospects to producers instantly, reducing response time from hours to minutes.

Self-Service Certificates of Insurance

Let certificate holders request and receive COIs through an automated portal — no CSR intervention needed. Reduce certificate turnaround from days to minutes with real-time validation.

AI Meeting & Call Summarization

Transcribe and summarize producer meetings, adjuster calls, and underwriting discussions automatically. Extract action items and push structured notes to your CRM or task management system.

Automated Client Data & Underwriting Portals

We replace static "digital brochure" websites with intelligent front-ends that capture structured prospect data in real time; validating inputs, scoring risk propensity via n8n webhooks, and persisting every submission to a PostgreSQL data warehouse. Moves leads from inquiry to review in seconds, not hours.

Who We Are

Insurance is a data orchestration problem, not a paperwork problem.

CRA was founded at the intersection of Actuarial Science and AI engineering. By combining SOA-trained risk modeling with modern automation infrastructure (n8n, Python, Docker, PostgreSQL), we replace manual tribal knowledge with structured, event-driven data systems. Whether it’s automating FNOL severity scoring, building intelligent lead capture portals, or executing lapse rate experience studies; we build the systems that let agencies scale without adding headcount.
