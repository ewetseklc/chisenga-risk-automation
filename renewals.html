How It Works

Upcoming Renewals Detected

The system identifies policies approaching expiry (90, 60, and 30 days out) and queues them for outreach.

Personalized messages are sent to each client automatically, whether by email, text, or both, with the right tone and timing.

Escalation & Tracking

Non-responders get escalated. Engagement is tracked. Your team sees a live dashboard of who renewed, who's at risk, and who needs a call.
